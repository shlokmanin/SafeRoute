🪪

Learner's Licence

A learner stage generally supports supervised learning before a full driving licence. Prepare by understanding road rules, signs and safe driving practices.

  • Understand eligibility concepts
  • Prepare for knowledge-style questions
  • Keep required documentation ready
  • Follow current official instructions
🚗

Driving Licence

The full-licence stage generally involves demonstrating driving ability and completing the applicable official process.

  • Build practical driving skills
  • Understand test expectations
  • Follow safe vehicle-control habits
  • Verify current procedure officially
🛵

Vehicle Types

Two-wheelers, four-wheelers and transport/commercial categories can have different requirements. Do not assume one category's process applies to another.

  • Two-Wheeler
  • Four-Wheeler
  • Transport / Commercial
Important: SafeRoute deliberately does not invent licence fees, age thresholds, validity periods, penalties or exact government procedures. Verify current requirements through official government/RTO sources.
